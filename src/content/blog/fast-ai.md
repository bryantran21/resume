### Status: In Progress 🚧

I'm currently working through the 2022/2023 version of the course. Unlike traditional academic courses that start with calculus, this starts with getting a model running in the first 30 minutes.

**Key Takeaways (so far):**
* **Top-down learning:** Implementation first, theory later.
* **The fastai library:** A high-level wrapper for PyTorch that makes training state-of-the-art models surprisingly accessible.
* **The Community:** The forums are an absolute goldmine for troubleshooting niche CUDA or architecture errors.

I'll be posting a full review and a repository of my Jupyter Notebooks once I've cleared the final lessons.
